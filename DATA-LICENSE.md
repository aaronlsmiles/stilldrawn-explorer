DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.23072784](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23072784). Source: [github.com/aaronlsmiles/stilldrawn](https://github.com/aaronlsmiles/stilldrawn).

## Changes made (CC BY 4.0 §3(a)(1)(B))

- Per-clip metric outputs are repackaged as compact JSON, and per-frame series are rounded to 4 significant figures.
- Poster stills (WebP) and frame-0 stills (PNG) are derived from the clips.
- **The clips themselves are unaltered**: byte-identical to the release, so their C2PA content credentials stay valid.

## Providers' notice

The release carries an informational notice (`NOTICE-PROVIDERS.md` in the source repository). It does not change the licence. It asks that these items not be used to train, fine-tune or improve generative image or video models:

- 9 panels and 5 ground-series variants made with Google Gemini 2.5 Flash Image via the Runway API
- 140 clips from Runway Gen-4 Turbo and Gen-4.5
- 148 clips from MiniMax H3 via fal
- 9 clips from Seedance

The media host blocks known AI crawlers to honour that request.
